He watched the water move beneath the boat.

The surface was almost perfectly still, broken only by the occasional ripple created by the paddle.

"Are you going to keep staring at it?"

David looked up.

The other person was already smiling.

"I was thinking."

"That's dangerous."

David laughed.

## II

The conversation had started easily enough, but neither of them mentioned what had happened the night before.

Instead, they talked about ordinary things.

The weather.

The city.

Where they would go next.

And, eventually, the conversation turned to the article David had been reading earlier.

{% capture excerpt %}

According to the report, the incident occurred shortly after midnight.

Several witnesses described hearing a loud sound coming from the direction of the river.

Authorities stated that there was no immediate explanation for what had happened.

{% endcapture %}

{% include excerpt.html content=excerpt %}

David folded the article and placed it beside him.

"Do you believe it?"

He didn't answer immediately.

{% capture definition %}

<strong>Paddling</strong> — The act of moving a boat through water using a paddle.

{% endcapture %}

{% include glossary.html content=definition %}

The word had always reminded him of summer.

It was strange how something so simple could bring back a memory he hadn't thought about in years.

{% include scene-break.html %}

## III

That evening, he opened an old journal.

{% capture journal %}

<strong>June 14</strong>

I went to the river again today.

The weather was warmer than I expected.

I thought about calling him, but decided against it.

Maybe tomorrow.

{% endcapture %}

{% include journal.html content=journal %}

He read the final line several times.

{% capture note %}

<strong>Don't forget.</strong>

Call me when you get there.

{% endcapture %}

{% include sticky-note.html content=note %}

For a moment, he simply stared at the note.

{% endcapture %}

{% include translator-note.html content=translator_note %}

He put the note away.

There were some things that were easier to leave unanswered.

{% include scene-break.html %}

## IV

The next morning, the river looked completely different.

The wind had picked up overnight.

He stood at the edge of the water and watched the waves.

For the first time, he wondered whether leaving had really been the right decision.

The thought stayed with him as he walked away.
